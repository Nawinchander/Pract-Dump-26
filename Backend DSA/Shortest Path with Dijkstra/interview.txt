Level 1

Why Dijkstra?

Because it finds the shortest path from a source to other vertices when edge weights are non-negative.

Level 2

Why not BFS?

BFS works for unweighted graphs or equal-weight edges.

Dijkstra handles different non-negative weights.


Level 3

Why Min Heap?

We need to efficiently retrieve the node having the smallest known distance.

Level 4

Can Dijkstra handle negative weights?

No.

For negative edge weights, consider Bellman-Ford.
